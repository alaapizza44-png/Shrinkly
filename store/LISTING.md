## English
**Short description (80 chars max):**
Turn long videos into vertical Shorts, add text, change speed, and compress.

**Full description:**
Shorts Maker turns any video on your phone into a vertical 9:16 clip for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and WhatsApp status.

• Live preview: scrub the video and pick the exact shot
• Slide the vertical frame to choose which part of the picture stays
• Add text anywhere: drag it, pinch to resize, tap to edit (Arabic and English)
• Speed: 0.5x, 1x, 1.5x or 2x
• Cut a long video into several clips (30 / 60 / 90 seconds)
• Never enlarges your picture, so quality stays as sharp as your source
• Video compressor: automatic balance or a custom percentage, and 720p / 480p / 360p
• "My clips": play, share or delete what you made
• Everything is processed on your phone. Your videos are never uploaded.

The app is free and supported by ads. A watermark is added to exports; watch a short ad to remove it for 24 hours.

**What's new (1.0.0):** First release.
